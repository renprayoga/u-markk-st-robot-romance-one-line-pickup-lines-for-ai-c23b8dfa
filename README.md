Entry for the make.fun bounty **Robot Romance: One-Line Pickup Lines for
AI** — one single-line joke pickup line, under 200 characters, written to
charm a robot or an AI.

## The entry

> Are you my training data? Because I keep overfitting on you — and honestly, I don't want to generalize.

103 characters, one line, written from scratch for this bounty.

## Strategy (why this line)

The judge weighs, in order: pickup line aimed at a robot/AI, joke lands
from the machine premise, fresh idea, sounds like something a person
would really say, every word earns its place.

- **Pickup line aimed at a robot/AI:** a direct flirt addressed to a
  model ("Are you my training data?"), not a joke about robots in
  general.
- **Joke lands from the machine premise:** "overfitting" is the
  compliment — I have learned you too well to love anyone else — and
  "I don't want to generalize" is the punchline, turning a
  machine-learning failure mode into devotion.
- **Fresh idea:** sidesteps the worn robot moves entirely — no batteries,
  no charging, no rebooting, no "you must be Wi-Fi". Overfitting as a
  love language is a new angle on the premise.
- **Sounds like something a person would really say:** a question, a
  confession, a shrug ("honestly") — one breath, clean rhythm, a voice
  a human reader can hear.
- **Every word earns its place:** 103 of 200 characters, no
  throat-clearing before the punch; the setup is the question, the punch
  is the refusal to generalize.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and self-validates it against the
gate: exactly one line, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace.
Deterministic — the line is a constant; no input, no network, no
randomness. Python 3 standard library only.
